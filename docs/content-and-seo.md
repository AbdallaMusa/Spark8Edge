# Research and implementation notes

- [ADMI courses](https://admi.africa/courses): puts duration, format and fee enquiries near course decisions. Spark8Edge’s training FAQ answers what it can without inventing dates, fees or accreditation.
- [Moringa School hiring](https://moringaschool.com/hire-our-graduates/): gives employers a dedicated route and answers hiring questions. Spark8Edge shows what to put in a brief without borrowing claims about a screened directory, placements or timelines.
- [Generation Kenya employers](https://kenya.generation.org/employers/): shows the order of steps an employer can expect. Spark8Edge explains the enquiry process and makes clear who chooses the candidate.
- [Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/organization): homepage Organization markup, with no speculative properties. Page-specific titles and inherited sharing imagery complement the existing canonical URLs and sitemap.

Implementation references: [SEO audit and copywriting](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills), [Vercel web guidelines](https://github.com/vercel-labs/web-interface-guidelines), [Vercel transition patterns](https://github.com/vercel-labs/agent-skills/tree/main/skills/react-view-transitions). The wordmark is CSS-only because it is an element reveal, not a page transition. The FAQ uses native disclosure controls and both effects respect reduced motion.
