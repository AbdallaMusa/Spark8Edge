# Spark8Edge product and marketing context

Spark8Edge is an early-stage Nairobi business offering one practical creative/digital training programme and building a talent network for project work. Organisations retain the final say on who they work with. Partnerships are the first homepage conversion priority; visitors should be able to message about partnerships, talent or training directly on WhatsApp.

## Claims and evidence
- Vanessa Mwando’s prior NGO experience is her background, not Spark8Edge’s client history. Use the supplied founder portrait and credited contextual imagery; never identify stock-photo subjects as participants.
- Course name, curriculum, fee, format and dates are not confirmed for publication. Do not imply an open cohort, placement guarantee, vetted directory or confirmed project roster.
- Publish practical working examples only as clearly labelled hypothetical briefs. No fabricated partners, testimonials, outcomes or impact metrics.
- The existing 2711 number is in the repo; confirm it is monitored before production promotion.

## Page jobs
Home: orient and open the partnership conversation. Training: help someone decide what to ask before enrolling. Talent: help an organisation write a brief and decide whether to proceed. Partnerships: explain possible roles and what to bring. About: human background and why the business exists.

## Learnings
Sparse slogans and repeated layouts felt blunt. Add concrete visitor-facing content and useful interactions rather than generic claims, wallpaper animation or decorative filler.
